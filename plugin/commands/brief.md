---
description: Show a short, scannable brief of what's on the agenda right now.
---

Call `agenda` with `view: "brief"`. Present the result as a short, scannable brief covering: today's appointments and reminders, anything overdue, follow-ups that need a nudge, and stale facts. Keep it compact — headings or a tight bullet list, not full sentences.

Do not call `notify` or send a push unless the user explicitly asks you to.
